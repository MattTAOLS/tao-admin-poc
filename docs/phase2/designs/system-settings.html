4Rules, severities and tolerances are read-only on screen. A change is a change to the controls, so it follows the existing path: "Request a change" records the rule, the proposed value and the reason in the Platform audit log and notifies the Lead; the Lead edits contracts/rules.yaml, make check and the rules tests run, and the new version shows here with its date.
