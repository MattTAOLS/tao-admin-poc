2Add user opens the drawer (b): the admin sets details, role and reports, and the person gets a sign-in email. Invite by email is the short form: email and role only, and the person fills in their own name on first sign-in; reports default to none until an admin sets them.
3"Reports received" summarises the subscription tree from (b): program, how many deals, and channels. It decides who gets Reports notifications in D7. Mentions reach a person whatever they receive.
(b) User management › Edit user (drawer) State after clicking "Edit" on P. Nguyen. "Add user" opens the same drawer empty, titled "Add user".
4A drawer from the right, so the table stays in view. Details and role: name, email (unique; changing it sends a confirmation to the new address), role TAO Admin or TAO Analyst. A TAO Admin cannot change their own role or deactivate themselves; there must always be at least one active TAO Admin.
5Status is Active or Deactivated. There is no hard delete: "Remove" deactivates. The person is signed out and cannot sign in; their uploads, overrides, comments and audit rows keep their name. Reactivate restores the same account.
6"Reports this user receives" is a tree of the 2 programs and their 12 deals. "All deals in this program, including new ones" ticks every deal and keeps any deal added later. Unticking it leaves the deals chosen one by one; new deals then are not added.
7Channel per program or per deal: in-app and email. In-app is ticked whenever the deal is. On a program row, the channels apply to all its deals unless a deal row says otherwise.
8Save writes one Platform audit row per change, with before and after (see the audit section below). History in the drawer shows this user's last changes.

10The tree lists only programs TAO has shared with this partner (today both MOVE Trust and F-PIL 2.0 are shared with Investor and Manager). If TAO stops sharing a program, it drops out of every teammate's tree and they stop receiving it.
