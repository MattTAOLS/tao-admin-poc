2Version history per covenant. History opens a panel under the row with every change to that deal and legal reference, newest first: who, when, old → new for each field that changed, the effective date, whether it was a new version or a correction, and the reason. The table gets a Last change column; the long Source text moves into the history panel and the Edit dialog, so the table fits without side-scrolling. Rows loaded from the seed show "Seed load".
3The change applies from the effective date; earlier periods keep the old version. R14 (Covenant thresholds equal the master record) picks the version in force on the last day of the report's collection period. In the sample above, reports for Dec 2026 and earlier keep ≤ 10.00×; Jan 2027 onward use ≤ 9.00×. The new row shows Scheduled until 2027-01-01, then In force, and the older row turns Superseded. Published reports are not re-checked or changed.

What this change does
  • Adds version 2 of SLFA 9.1 (i): Ceiling ≤ 9.00× from 2027-01-01. Version 1 (≤ 10.00× from 2022-12-09) stays in force until then.
  • R14 uses version 2 for collection periods from Jan 2027. Reports for Dec 2026 and earlier, published or in review, are not affected.
  • Reports in review now: Moneda Capital Corp. Aug 2026 uses version 1. No re-check needed.
  • Platform audit log: "Covenant changed", with old and new values and your reason.
4New version or correction. A new version needs a later effective date and leaves past periods alone. A correction fixes a wrongly entered record, keeps the same effective date, and re-runs R14 on every report of that deal still in review; published reports are not changed (to change one, use Correct a value in D1). Both need a reason. The button label follows the choice: Save new version or Save correction.
5Fields. Direction (ceiling or floor), threshold (single or banded, as today), unit, watch buffer, effective from, source, and the new required reason. Deal and legal reference are fixed when editing; change them by adding a new covenant. The impact box is worked out from the dates before saving, so TAO Admin sees which reports are affected.
